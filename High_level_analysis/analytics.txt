The directory contains analysis at the to level for all the companies across pharmaceuticals, device manufacturers. 
Physicians, non-physician practitioners are involved in research and clinical trials.

- The dataset comprises of general payments and research payments. The companies provide medical devices, medical supplies for conducting research. 

Phramaceutical, biomedical device manufacturers conduct research, drug discovery, clinical trials and studies of long term impacts. 

- Research payments: Research institutions like National institute of health, Memorial Sloan Kettering cancer center, Duke university. 

- General payments: The trials are conducted in teaching hospitals like Dana-Farber cancer institute, Massachusetts General hospital, Brigham and women’s hospital. 

The physicians conducting trials speak in public forums and medical institutions for educational purposes. 
They are compensated in various ways like stock options, cash, in kind items and services. 
The nature of payment varies by purpose. Few examples are travel, food and beverages, public speaking, device or medical supply.
